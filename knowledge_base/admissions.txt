ADMISSION PROCESS

According to the college admission page:

1. Online Registration
   Students can create an account on the VSB Admissions Portal and fill in the application form.

2. Application Fee Payment
   The admission page states that the application fee is ₹100 and is non-refundable.

3. Seat Confirmation
   Students accept the allocated seat and pay the admission fee to confirm enrollment.

4. Document Upload
   Required documents such as mark sheets, identity proof, photographs and certificates are uploaded.

5. Counseling
   Students attend online or offline counseling for course selection and seat allocation based on merit.

6. Document Verification
   Original documents are submitted for verification at the college campus or through authorized centres.

ADMISSION QUOTA

The college admission page states that admissions are made according to State Government norms.

65% of seats are filled through Single Window Counselling.

35% of seats are filled through the Consortium of Self-Financing Professional Colleges, Tamil Nadu, according to Government of Tamil Nadu norms.

The college also states that reservation follows the TNEA Reservation Policy.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Merit scholarships are available.

Students with more than 190 cutoff marks:
100% tuition fee waiver
50% hostel fee and college bus fee free

Students with 185 to 189.75 cutoff marks:
100% tuition fee waiver

Students with 180 to 184.75 cutoff marks:
50% tuition fee waiver

The college states that scholarships have been awarded to 1,094 students during the last three academic years.
