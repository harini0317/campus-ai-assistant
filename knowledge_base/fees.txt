V.S.B ENGINEERING COLLEGE – FEES AND SCHOLARSHIPS

College:
V.S.B Engineering College, Karur

FEE STRUCTURE

V.S.B Engineering College states that tuition fees are charged according to the fee prescribed by the State Level Fee Committee.

UNDERGRADUATE (UG) TUITION FEE

Government Quota:

Accredited UG Course:
₹55,000 per year

Non-Accredited UG Course:
₹50,000 per year

Management Quota:

Accredited UG Course:
₹87,500 per year

Non-Accredited UG Course:
₹85,000 per year

POSTGRADUATE (PG) TUITION FEE

Government Quota:

Accredited PG Course:
₹30,000 per semester

Non-Accredited PG Course:
₹25,000 per semester

Management Quota:

Accredited PG Course:
₹30,000 per semester

Non-Accredited PG Course:
₹25,000 per semester

IMPORTANT

The exact fee applicable to a student depends on the course, accreditation status and admission quota.

Students should confirm the applicable fee with the college admission office before making payment.

SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT

V.S.B Engineering College provides merit-based scholarships and financial support.

MERIT SCHOLARSHIP

Students with more than 190 cutoff marks:

• 100% tuition fee waiver
• 50% hostel fee free
• College bus fee free

Students with 185 to 189.75 cutoff marks:

• 100% tuition fee waiver

Students with 180 to 184.75 cutoff marks:

• 50% tuition fee waiver

SCHOLARSHIP INFORMATION

The college states that scholarships have been awarded to 1,094 students during the last three academic years.

FEE PAYMENT

The official admissions page provides the tuition fee structure but does not specify all payment schedules, late-payment penalties or detailed payment methods.

For the latest payment instructions, students should contact the college admission office.
