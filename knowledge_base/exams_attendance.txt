V.S.B ENGINEERING COLLEGE – EXAMINATIONS AND ATTENDANCE

ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT

Attendance is taken at the beginning of every period.

Students who come late to class or are unavailable when attendance is taken are considered absent for that session.

Students should normally maintain the required attendance prescribed by the academic regulations.

MINIMUM ATTENDANCE

A student should have overall attendance of at least 75% in a semester to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

ATTENDANCE BETWEEN 65% AND 75%

Students having overall attendance between 65% and 75% may be permitted to appear for the semester examination in eligible cases.

This relaxation is applicable for reasons such as:

• Hospitalization
• Accident
• Specific illness
• Participation in College, University, State, National or International level sports events

For medical reasons, the student must submit the required medical certificate.

For sports participation, the student must submit the sports participation certificate.

The required permission and documents are forwarded to the Controller of Examinations.

ATTENDANCE BELOW 65%

Students having less than 65% overall attendance are not permitted to appear for the end-semester examination.

Such students are also not permitted to move to the next semester and may have to repeat the incomplete semester according to the applicable readmission norms.

COURSE-WISE ATTENDANCE

Students must also satisfy the attendance requirement for individual courses.

If a student does not satisfy the course-wise attendance requirement, the student will not be permitted to write that particular course in the end-semester examination.

The student must register for the course again in a subsequent semester and fulfil the attendance requirement.

EXAMINATION SYSTEM

The college conducts Continuous Internal Assessment Tests and End-Semester Examinations through the Office of the Controller of Examinations.

The Controller of Examinations is responsible for:

• Preparing examination timetables
• Exam hall arrangements
• Invigilator arrangements
• Conducting Continuous Internal Assessment Tests
• Conducting End-Semester Examinations
• Processing and publishing results
• Issuing grade sheets and other examination certificates

INSTITUTIONAL TESTS

According to the college student policy, a semester includes:

• Two Unit Tests
• Three Internal Tests
• One Model Examination

The Unit Tests and Internal Tests have a duration of 100 minutes.

The Model Examination has a duration of 3 hours.

ATTENDANCE FOR INTERNAL TESTS

Students are expected to attend institutional tests compulsorily.

Absence from institutional tests is treated seriously.

Students who fail to obtain the minimum pass mark of 60% in the three internal tests are required to attend internal retests.

INTERNAL ASSESSMENT

The college follows a transparent internal assessment process.

Internal assessment schedules are prepared according to the applicable university/academic regulations.

Internal marks and attendance percentage are periodically communicated to students and parents.

Answer scripts are valued and verified by the concerned department.

Any changes in the evaluation scheme or examination pattern are communicated to students.

EXAMINATION REGISTRATION

A student must satisfy the required attendance conditions and register for the examination by paying the prescribed examination fee before appearing for the end-semester examination.

REVALUATION

The Controller of Examinations provides application facilities for examination-related processes including revaluation.

Students should follow the latest instructions and deadlines issued by the Controller of Examinations for revaluation.

IMPORTANT

Examination rules and academic regulations may be updated according to the applicable regulations of the college and affiliating/academic authorities.

Students should always check the latest examination timetable, regulations and notices issued by the Office of the Controller of Examinations.
