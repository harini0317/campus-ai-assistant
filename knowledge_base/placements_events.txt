PLACEMENT TRAINING

Students receive career development and placement support through the Career Development Center.

Placement preparation can include activities related to:

• Aptitude preparation
• Technical preparation
• Interview preparation
• Career guidance
• Company recruitment processes
• Placement drives

Students should follow the latest instructions and eligibility criteria announced by the Career Development Center for each recruitment drive.

IMPORTANT PLACEMENT NOTE

Company names, number of offers, salary packages and recruitment opportunities can change from year to year.

The salary figures published by the college represent the placement information for the respective batch/year and should not be treated as a guaranteed salary for every student.

Students should check the latest CDC/placement announcements for current recruitment drives and eligibility.

CAMPUS ACTIVITIES

V.S.B Engineering College provides opportunities for students to participate in academic, technical, cultural, sports and other campus activities.

The college has student clubs and activity-based opportunities that support student participation, technical learning and overall development.

EVENTS AND ACTIVITIES

Students can participate in activities such as:

• Technical events
• Workshops
• Seminars
• Training programs
• Student club activities
• Cultural activities
• Sports activities

The exact events, dates and registration procedures may change each academic year.

Students should check the latest college announcements for upcoming events.

LIBRARY

V.S.B Engineering College provides library and learning-resource facilities for students.

The college provides access to academic resources to support teaching, learning and research.

For current library timings, borrowing limits, renewal rules and digital-resource access, students should check the latest library instructions issued by the college.
