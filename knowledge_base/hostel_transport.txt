V.S.B ENGINEERING COLLEGE – HOSTEL AND TRANSPORT

HOSTEL

V.S.B Engineering College provides hostel facilities for students.

The college campus has separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.

Hostel facilities are provided to support students who require accommodation during their studies.

HOSTEL FACILITIES

The college provides residential facilities and supporting amenities for hostel students.

Students staying in the hostel should follow the hostel rules and instructions issued by the college and hostel administration.

HOSTEL ADMISSION

Students who require hostel accommodation should contact the college hostel administration for availability, admission procedure, hostel fees and other requirements.

Hostel availability and charges may vary depending on the academic year.

HOSTEL RULES

Students staying in the hostel are expected to follow the rules and regulations issued by the hostel administration.

Specific details such as hostel entry timings, outing permissions, visitor rules and leave procedures should be confirmed with the hostel administration.

MESS AND FOOD

Hostel students are provided with food facilities through the hostel mess.

The latest mess arrangements, menu, mess fees and timings should be confirmed with the hostel administration.

TRANSPORT

V.S.B Engineering College provides college bus/transport facilities for students.

College buses are available for students travelling to and from the college.

TRANSPORT FACILITIES

Students who use college transport should follow the routes, timings and instructions announced by the college transport administration.

Bus routes and timings may change depending on the academic year and operational requirements.

BUS FEES

Transport charges depend on the applicable route and current college fee structure.

Students should contact the college transport office for the latest bus routes, availability and fee details.

IMPORTANT

The official college website confirms the availability of hostel and transport facilities, but specific details such as:

• Hostel fee
• Mess fee
• Hostel timings
• Outing/leave timings
• Bus route-wise timings
• Bus route-wise fees
• Hostel room allocation
• Warden contact details

should be confirmed with the college because these details may change.
